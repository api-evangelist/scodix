Digital embellishment helps web-to-print providers transform personalized print into premium, tactile products. By integrating effects such as digital foil, spot varnish, raised details and textures into an automated production workflow, providers can offer higher-value products without relying on dies, screens or labor-intensive finishing processes. For web-to-print businesses, the opportunity goes beyond adding visual effects.
